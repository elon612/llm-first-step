Once upon a time there was a little cat. The little cat loved milk.
The little cat sat on the mat. The mat was warm and soft.
The dog saw the little cat. The dog and the cat played in the sun.
The sun was bright. The little cat drank the milk and slept on the mat.
People asked the cat many questions. The cat did not answer with words.
The cat answered with a purr. A purr is a small sound of comfort.
If you ask a language model a question, it predicts the next word.
Then it predicts the next word again. That is how a story grows.
A large language model is a next-token machine. It does not look up facts
the way a database does. It continues the pattern in the prompt.

从前有一只小猫。小猫喜欢牛奶。小猫坐在垫子上。垫子又暖又软。
狗看见了小猫。狗和猫在太阳下玩耍。太阳很亮。小猫喝了牛奶，睡在垫子上。
人们问猫很多问题。猫不用词语回答。猫用呼噜回答。呼噜是一种舒服的小声音。
如果你问语言模型一个问题，它会预测下一个词。然后再预测下一个词。故事就是这样长出来的。
大语言模型是一台“下一个 token”机器。它不像数据库那样查找事实。它是在续写提示词里的模式。
